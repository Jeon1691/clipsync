# Code of conduct

This policy covers ClipSync issues, pull requests, reviews and other official
project discussions. Treat participants respectfully, welcome newcomers, focus
criticism on technical work, and respect people's privacy and boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, publication
of private information, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Do not use
project spaces to pressure someone into sharing credentials or private data.

The conduct contact is [Jeon1691](https://github.com/Jeon1691). Report a concern
through an existing private contact, or open an issue requesting a private
contact without including sensitive details or identifying the affected person.
For a software vulnerability, use the separate process in SECURITY.md.

The maintainer reviews available evidence, gives affected participants a chance
to respond where appropriate, and may request a correction, remove content,
restrict participation or block an account. Actions should be proportionate to
the behavior and its impact. Reports and personal information are kept private
except where disclosure is necessary to investigate or protect participants.
Request reconsideration with additional evidence through the same contact.

The project currently has one maintainer and no independent conduct panel.
Reports about the maintainer have that limitation; GitHub's platform reporting
tools remain available for violations of GitHub's own policies.
